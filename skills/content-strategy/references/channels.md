# Channels: roles, cadence, formats

Every number here (frequency, length, timing) is a **starting point, not a verified fact**. Platform behaviour changes.
Test against the subject's own results and check the platform's current help pages before stating a limit as fact.

## Roles

Assign each channel in `{{channels}}` one role and a reason. Template:

| Channel | Role | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `{{home_channel}}` | Flagship. Authority, proof, and the page traffic or outreach lands on | Where `{{icp}}` already is |
| Short-text channel (for example X) | Fast takes and conversation; cuts of flagship ideas | Short half-life rewards frequency |
| Visual channel (for example Instagram) | Carousels and short video from the strongest ideas | Reach and a human side |
| Video channel (for example YouTube) | Short explainers from the best ideas | Longest shelf life; searchable |
| Newsletter | Depth for people who opted in | Owned audience |

Pick the home channel from where the audience is and where the subject's outreach or sales happens. State the **cut
order** for when time drops, always protecting the home channel.

## Sizing to weekly hours

Ask for `{{hours_per_week}}`, then choose a tier. These tiers are a heuristic.

| Hours per week | Plan |
|---|---|
| 3 to 4 | Home channel 2 posts a week plus a daily 10-minute commenting routine. One secondary channel as rewrites of those posts. Nothing else. |
| 5 to 7 | Home channel 2 to 3 posts a week plus commenting. Secondary text channel 3 to 5 short posts a week. One visual piece a week. |
| 8 or more | The above plus a video every one or two weeks. |

Quality beats quantity. Three strong posts beat seven weak ones. Skip a slot when there is nothing real to say.

## Per-channel notes

### Home channel (long-form text, for example LinkedIn)

- Lead with proof or a position, not a topic. The opening must work before any "see more" fold (verify the current fold
  length).
- Rotate formats: text post, document carousel, short story post, occasional poll.
- Comments are the relationship engine: reply to every comment, add substantive comments on posts by peers and target
  accounts. Keep a list of target accounts.
- External links may reduce reach. Treat it as a test: try the link in the first comment and compare.
- Promotional posts are rare and earned.

### Short-text channel (for example X)

- Short, specific takes; threads for deeper ideas. The first line must stand alone.
- Re-hook for the channel. Do not paste the flagship.
- Reply to people in the field. Replies build visibility faster than posting into silence.

### Visual channel (carousels and short video)

- Carousels from the clearest framework or list in a flagship: one idea per slide, short text, hook on slide one.
- Short video: hook in the first seconds, on-screen text (many people watch muted), one clear point.
- Keep design simple and consistent. Function first, polish later.

### Video channel (AI-assisted or screen-recorded)

- Respect `{{off_limits}}`: if video production is a named gap, position this as AI-assisted video and never imply
  production skill.
- **Default production mode: not on camera.** Pick one per video and say which: (1) narration over AI-generated or
  stock visuals with on-screen text, (2) screen recording with narration, (3) text on screen only. Write a talking-head
  script only on request.
- **Script format:** scene by scene, each with a narration line, a visual (an AI image or video prompt, or the
  screen-recording step), and the text overlay. Claims must be identical to the source post. AI visuals must not invent
  results, product screens or customers.
- **Labelling:** mark which scenes use AI visuals or voice, so the platform's current disclosure setting can be applied.
- Searchable titles and a clear promise in the first lines. Start with short explainers before committing to a series.

### Newsletter

- One idea per issue, taken from a flagship. Depth the social cuts cannot hold. Plain subject line that states the
  promise.

## Per-channel don'ts

- Do not post identical text across channels. Rewrite the hook and rhythm for each.
- No unsourced statistics. No engagement-bait closers ("Thoughts?") or manufactured vulnerability.
- No publishing without a reviewed draft. The subject approves and posts.
