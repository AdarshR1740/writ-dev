# Calendar and measurement

## Weekly batch workflow

Batching by format is cheaper than creating daily. Adjust to `{{hours_per_week}}`. The sizes are a sketch.

| Session | Output |
|---|---|
| Ideation (30 to 45 min) | Pick this week's 1 or 2 flagships from the backlog; check material in the story bank |
| Writing | Flagship drafts by the subject, then the cuts |
| Visuals | Carousel slides and graphics |
| Video | Scripts and assets (only if video is in the plan) |
| Review | The subject reads, edits and approves everything; Writ's grammar and fact checks run first |
| Engage | Daily 10 to 15 minutes: replies on own posts, comments on target accounts |

Leave one or two open slots per week for reacting to something real.

## Status ladder

Every item moves through: `idea → drafted → reviewed → approved → posted → measured`. Only the subject moves an item to
`approved` and `posted`. Rejected items are dropped, not kept in limbo.

In Writ, `reviewed` and `approved` correspond to the checks and the sign-off on the piece, so the ladder is the
editorial record, not a separate checklist.

## Four-week calendar format

| Date | Channel | Pillar | Format | Hook | Source (story bank, trend, ...) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules: no pillar or format stacked on consecutive days on the same channel; every flagship has its cuts scheduled
after it; at most one promotional post a week; open slots marked.

## What to measure

Judge content by what it does for `{{goal}}`. Likes alone do not tell you that. Pick the signals that match the goal.

| Signal | Why it matters | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Profile or page visits, and who the visitors are | Are the right people looking? | Platform analytics |
| Inbound messages and connection or contact requests | Direct evidence of authority | Platform and manual count |
| Reply or conversion rate on outreach | Does the profile convert curiosity? | The outreach log |
| Saves and substantive comments | Quality of resonance | Platform analytics |
| Performance by channel against hours spent | Which channels are worth the time | Platform analytics |

Record baselines first. Targets without a baseline are guesses, so set them only after two to four weeks of data and
mark them *assumed*.

## Weekly review (15 minutes)

1. Top 2 and bottom 2 posts. For each: pillar, format, hook type, and a one-line guess at why. Mark the guess as a
   guess.
2. Did outreach replies or visits move after any post?
3. Was the plan sustainable? If slots were missed, cut scope, not guilt.
4. Pick what to repeat, what to drop and what to test next week (one change at a time).

## Monthly review

- Pillar mix against the plan.
- Channels: keep, cut or grow, using hours per result.
- Profile alignment: does the headline, About or landing page still match what the subject says in outreach?
- Update the positioning line only if the situation changed. Suggest durable changes to the house file; write only
  after the user approves.

## Stop rules

- If a post is being forced to fill a slot, skip it. Nobody notices an absent post, and weak ones cost trust.
- If a channel produces nothing after a fair trial (set the trial length together), pause it rather than defend it.
