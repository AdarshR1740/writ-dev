# Writ: brand notes

## Name
**Writ.** A writ is a formal written document issued under someone's authority and
seal. The name keeps the root of "write", and the meaning is the product: content
that a named expert stands behind and signs off.

- Always "Writ", capital W. Never "Writ AI", "WRIT" or "writ.".
- AI appears in the descriptor, not the name: the promise is expert-owned content,
  and AI is how the legwork gets done.
- Still to do before launch: a trademark search and a domain. A company named Writ
  exists in Cincinnati (business unknown).

## Lines
- Descriptor: **Expert content, AI-assisted.**
- Sign-in line: *AI does the legwork. Your experts own the substance and the sign-off.*

## Mark
A written page with a seal breaking its lower-right corner: the document and the
sign-off in one shape.

- Source: `web/src/components/Logo.tsx` (`LogoMark`, `Wordmark`) and
  `web/public/favicon.svg`. The home-screen icon is `web/public/apple-touch-icon.png`.
- The page and its two lines follow the theme's ink and surface colours; the seal is
  always the pencil blue. The gap around the seal is a mask cut, not a stroke, so the
  mark sits on any background.
- Minimum size 16px, where the lines still read. Keep clear space of a quarter of the
  mark's width around it.
- Wordmark: Geist 700, letter-spacing -0.04em, the mark at about 1.2× the cap height.

## Colour
| Role | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Ink (page, text) | `#1b2230` | `#e6e9ee` |
| Paper (background) | `#f5f6f8` | `#12161d` |
| Pencil (seal, links, actions) | `#2f4bd8` | `#93a5ff` |
| Insert / approved | `#145c38` on `#ddf3e6` | `#9be3b8` on `#173a28` |
| Needs attention | `#94570a` on `#fbf0dc` | `#f0c27a` on `#3a2c14` |

Green means done or approved, amber means act on this, pencil blue means a link or
something to decide, and neutral grey means advice.

## Type
- **Geist** for the app and headings.
- **Source Serif 4** for the writing itself: documents, research claims, quotes.
- **Geist Mono** for IDs, versions and citation numbers only.

## Voice
Plain, direct and specific, like a good editor. Say what happened and what to do
next. No hype, no exclamation marks, and no claims the tool can't back up: the same
standard Writ holds content to.
